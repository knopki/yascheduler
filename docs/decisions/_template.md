## Context

The problem, constraint, or force that required a decision. What would happen
if no decision were made. The viable options that were considered, summarized
briefly (1-2 lines each). Only enough for a future reader to understand the
shape of the trade-off.

## Decision

What we decided. One or two declarative sentences. No alternatives here —
they live in Context. If the decision has a precise form (a protocol, a type
signature, a boundary), state it concretely.
